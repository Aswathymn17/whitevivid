🌿 WHITE VIVID HOMESTAY 🌿

A Quiet Escape,Thoughtfully Designed

White Vivid Homestay is a premium and peaceful homestay in Kuttikanam, Kerala, offering a perfect blend of comfort, nature, and privacy. Surrounded by lush greenery and mist-covered hills, it is an ideal choice for travelers seeking a peaceful stay in Vagamon away from the rush of city life.

Conveniently located near popular Vagamon tourist attractions, our homestay is suitable for families, couples, and groups looking for a relaxing and memorable getaway.

Our rooms are spacious, clean, and thoughtfully arranged to ensure a comfortable stay. Guests can wake up to scenic hill views, enjoy the cool mountain breeze, and experience a calm, refreshing atmosphere throughout their stay.
